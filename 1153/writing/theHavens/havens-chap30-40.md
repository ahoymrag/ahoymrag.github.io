## on homeless

When I first moved here - I was charmed by those - I didn't know a soul - most of my friends - lest the folks that I would talk to every day - were indeed homeless. I met Marcus, Henry, etc - all that way. And to what end. Something now has shifted - having living here 6 years - whereas I no longer find them charming. For they are not jolly - they seem desperate - for okay - give them what they want - get them land - stop trying to build affordable housing in this city - it's never going to be affordable to someone who makes less than 1k - okay - so put them out of the land - and make this be a goal if they so choose - or let them live in a box out in the distance lands. For I see no path in there present state of how they could make it out.

But this is all a wash and a rambling - perhaps I am getting older.
