TMP Rosé

It’s a rare rosé blend that’s crisp and dry, pairs well with charcuterie and tomato-based dishes. On the nose you’ll find strawberry, peach, floral undertones with a bit of minerals. Highly drinkable, best enjoyed chilled.

Montauk Project

The Montauk Project was inspired through the passion felt by two friends as an homage to and for the most scenic part of NY. Fashioned with French sensibility and elegance, we bring you a foreign import, domestically.
