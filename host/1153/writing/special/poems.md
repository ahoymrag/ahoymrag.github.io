7.5.23

on a plane from New York
headed to the London
wondering what my time would be like
if I had any wonder for the time
for I feel like an immortal 
living in a mortal body
help Lord help 
put me in place
show me your grace
as I seek your face
may it be true
may it please be you 
for I want
yet I put these other things
in your way 
and forget so quick
that today - is the day - that you have made
giving too much presure to myself
if I can't blame me - how can I blame anybody else
