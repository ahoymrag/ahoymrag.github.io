Explanation of Long-term Predictions

The long-term predictions are calculated using the average monthly change percentage derived from historical data. This percentage is compounded over the specified number of years to estimate future net worth.

Example Calculations:

Suppose the average monthly change is 1.5%. To predict the net worth for different years, the formula used is:

Future Value = Current Value × (1 + Average Change/100)^(12 × Number of Years)

For a current value of $100,000, the calculations would be:

5 Years:  Future Value = $100,000 × (1.015)^60 ≈ $209,378.14
10 Years: Future Value = $100,000 × (1.015)^120 ≈ $438,391.15
15 Years: Future Value = $100,000 × (1.015)^180 ≈ $918,998.45
20 Years: Future Value = $100,000 × (1.015)^240 ≈ $1,926,824.92
30 Years: Future Value = $100,000 × (1.015)^360 ≈ $9,292,354.54

This means the predicted net worth after 5, 10, 15, 20, and 30 years would be approximately $209,378.14, $438,391.15, $918,998.45, $1,926,824.92, and $9,292,354.54 respectively.
