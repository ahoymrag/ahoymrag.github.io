A crypto solution for indie film funding

The Problem

Indie films represent over 7% of the $95.45 billion film industry yet remain drastically underserved in funding.

The Solution

Cinema.fi incentivizes the funding of indie films by offering blockchain-based ownership of film parts.

How It Works

Filmmakers post their projects; investors fund them and receive NFTs representing parts of the film.

The Deep Dive

Investors receive tokens reflecting their contribution, convertible into NFT frames upon film completion.

Post Production & Secondary Market

Trade film frame NFTs on the open market, supporting both filmmakers and the platform through transaction fees.
