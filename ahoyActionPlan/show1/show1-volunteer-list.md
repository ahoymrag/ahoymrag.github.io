## Volunteer/Staff Roles

### Event Coordinator
- **Responsibilities**: Oversee the entire event, coordinate with all teams, handle any issues that arise.

### Technical Crew
- **Responsibilities**: Set up and manage sound and lighting equipment, troubleshoot technical issues.

### Marketing Team
- **Responsibilities**: Promote the event, manage social media, handle press releases.

### Hospitality Team
- **Responsibilities**: Greet guests, manage seating, provide assistance as needed.

### Security Team
- **Responsibilities**: Ensure the safety of guests and performers, manage crowd control.

### Cleanup Crew
- **Responsibilities**: Clean up the venue after the event, ensure all equipment is returned.
