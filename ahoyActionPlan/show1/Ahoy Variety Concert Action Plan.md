## Timeline for the Night

- **6:00 PM** - Doors Open
  - Guests arrive, find seats, and enjoy pre-show music.

- **7:00 PM** - Opening Remarks
  - MC Rob Meglio welcomes guests and introduces the event.

- **8:45 PM** - First Act: The Tines
  - Performance by The Tines.

- **10:00 PM** - Intermission
  - 15-minute break for guests to mingle and refresh.

- **10:15 PM** - Second Act: Jake Custer
  - Performance by Jake Custer.

- **11:00 PM** - Dance Party
  - Guests can enjoy a dance party.

- **12:00 AM** - Event Ends
  - Guests depart.

## 30-Day Preparation Timeline

### Day 1-5: Initial Planning
- Confirm lineup and MC.
- Secure venue (Cafe 9).
- Draft and send out invitations.

### Day 6-10: Marketing and Promotion
- Create promotional materials (flyers, social media posts).
- Reach out to local media and influencers.
- Send out press releases.

### Day 11-15: Logistics and Coordination
- Arrange sound and lighting equipment.
- Coordinate with performers for technical requirements.
- Plan seating arrangements and ticketing.

### Day 16-20: Final Preparations
- Confirm all bookings and arrangements.
- Conduct a walkthrough of the venue.
- Finalize the event schedule and timeline.

### Day 21-25: Rehearsals and Final Checks
- Schedule rehearsals with performers.
- Test sound and lighting equipment.
- Finalize the guest list and send reminders.

### Day 26-30: Event Execution
- Set up the venue.
- Conduct a final rehearsal.
- Ensure all staff and volunteers are briefed and ready.
