## Ministry Responsibilities & Roles

- Develop high-quality production practices for streamed services, archived recorded services, and podcast creation.
- Onsite execution of recording and streaming weekly Sunday worship services, weekly Wednesday Night Compline services, School of Discipleship podcast, and occasional special services.
- Post-production work for recorded services and podcasts including graphic design on video content, audio/video editing, and timely uploads of content upon completion.
- Further develop the vision and mission of CPC’s online presence in recorded archived service content and podcast content.
- Maintains audio/visual equipment owned by the church.
- Recruits, trains, and manages CPC’s A/V team volunteers in best practices, equipment operation, etc.
- Oversees CPC A/V budget in conjunction with members of the A/V Production Team.

## 2023 Outlook
