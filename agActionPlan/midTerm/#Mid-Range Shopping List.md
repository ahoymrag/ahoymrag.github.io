### Steps to Assemble

1. **Secure the Yakima Universal MightyMount to the Roof Rack**
   - **Position the MightyMount:** Place the MightyMount on your roof rack’s crossbars.
   - **Attach the MightyMount:** Use the provided mounting hardware to secure the MightyMount to the roof rack.

2. **Attach the NICEYRIG Camera Cheese Mounting Plate to the MightyMount**
   - **Position the Adapter Plate:** Place the NICEYRIG Cheese Plate on top of the MightyMount.
   - **Insert the Bolts:** Insert M6 bolts through the aligned holes of the adapter plate and the MightyMount.
   - **Secure the Adapter Plate:** Use M6 nuts and washers to fasten the adapter plate to the MightyMount.

3. **Attach the Manfrotto Tripod Head to the Adapter Plate**
   - **Position the Tripod Head:** Place the Manfrotto MVH502AH Pro Video Head on the adapter plate.
   - **Insert the Screws:** Use the 1/4”-20 screws to attach the tripod head to the adapter plate.
   - **Tighten the Screws:** Use a screwdriver or Allen key to tighten the screws securely.

4. **Add Vibration Dampening Pads (Optional)**
   - **Place the Pads:** Insert the Isolate IT Sorbothane Pads between the adapter plate and the MightyMount or between the roof rack and the MightyMount.
   - **Secure Everything:** Ensure all connections are tight and secure to reduce vibrations.
