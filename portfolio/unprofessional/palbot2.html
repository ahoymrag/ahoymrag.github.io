Pal-bot 2

Project Overview

About the Project

"Pal-bot 2" is a film project where I had the opportunity to co-direct, produce, edit, and color. This project was a significant undertaking from 2018 and was successfully released in 2020.

My Role and Contributions

  • Co-Director: Collaborated in the directorial vision and decision-making process with director Rob Meglio.
  • Producer: Managed the production, overseeing the project from crew to completion.
  • Editor: Responsible for editing, and responsible for the film's narrative flow and pace.
  • Colorist: Enhanced the visual appeal through color grading, setting the tone and mood of the film.
