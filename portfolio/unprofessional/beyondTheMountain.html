About the Project

"Beyond the Mountain" is an immersive Virtual Reality (VR) film that takes viewers on an extraordinary journey through mystical landscapes and enchanting narratives. As the first VR film to be accepted into the Middlebury Film Festival, it represents a groundbreaking step in VR storytelling. Directed by the talented Alex Gonzalez, this film blends captivating visuals with interactive elements, inviting the audience into a world where fantasy and reality merge. The project not only showcases the potential of VR in filmmaking but also Alex's exceptional ability to craft engaging, interactive experiences.
