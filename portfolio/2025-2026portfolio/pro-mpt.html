pro-mpt

A prompt archive for capturing human questions, ideas, and thinking in the age of AI.

The idea

pro-mpt explores what it means to keep a record of the prompts people write with AI: not only the outputs, but the questions, intentions, revisions, and patterns of thought behind them.

Concept and local-first tool · In development

Documented capabilities

  • Log prompts with model, app, domain, version, and rating metadata
  • Search, list, and review an archive of prompts
  • Generate expertise summaries and export Markdown journals
  • Import and watch supported local conversation sources

Stack

Python · Typer · Rich · SQLite · Anthropic API
