Grilled Baby Back Ribs Recipe

This guide will show you how to prepare and grill baby back ribs that are flavorful and tender.

Ingredients

  • 2 racks baby back ribs (about 4-5 pounds)
  • Your favorite dry rub
  • Barbecue sauce (for basting and serving)

Preparation

  1. Remove the membrane from the back of each rack of ribs. Slide a knife under the membrane and over a bone to lift and loosen it. With a paper towel, grab the edge of the membrane and pull it off.
  2. Apply a generous amount of dry rub to both sides of the ribs, patting it in to ensure it adheres well.

Grilling Instructions

  1. Preheat your grill to 250 degrees F (120 degrees C) for indirect grilling.
  2. Place the ribs meat-side up on the cooler part of the grill. Cover the grill and cook for about 2-3 hours.
  3. Check the ribs for doneness. The ribs are done when the meat is tender and shrinks back from the ends of the bones.
  4. About 30 minutes before the ribs are done, start basting with your barbecue sauce every 10 minutes.
  5. Remove the ribs from the grill and let them rest for at least 10 minutes. Cut between the bones to separate the ribs and serve with additional barbecue sauce.

Tips

Keep the grill temperature steady at 250 degrees F to ensure the ribs cook evenly. Avoid opening the grill too often to check the ribs, as this can cause significant heat loss.
