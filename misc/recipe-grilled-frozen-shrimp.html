Grilled Frozen Shrimp Recipe

Learn how to grill frozen shrimp with a simple and delicious marinade.

Ingredients

  • 1 pound frozen shrimp, peeled and deveined
  • 2 tablespoons olive oil
  • 1 teaspoon garlic powder
  • 1 teaspoon paprika
  • Salt and pepper to taste
  • Lemon wedges (for serving)

Instructions

  1. Preheat your grill to medium-high heat.
  2. Rinse the frozen shrimp under cold water to remove any ice glaze and pat them dry with paper towels.
  3. In a bowl, mix olive oil, garlic powder, paprika, salt, and pepper.
  4. Toss the shrimp in the marinade until they are well-coated.
  5. Thread the shrimp onto skewers, piercing once near the tail and once near the head.
  6. Place the skewers on the grill and cook for about 2-3 minutes on each side or until the shrimp are opaque and slightly charred.
  7. Serve hot with lemon wedges on the side.

Tips

Using olive oil in the marinade helps prevent the shrimp from sticking to the grill and adds a rich flavor. Make sure the grill is hot before adding the shrimp to achieve a nice sear.
