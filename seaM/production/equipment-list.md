# Equipment List for "Sea Monster" Shoot

## Camera
- **Blackmagic Pocket Cinema Camera 4K (BMPCC4K)**
  - 4K DCI recording
  - Dual native ISO for low light performance

## Lenses
- **Anamorphic Lenses**
  - **SIRUI 50mm f/1.8 Anamorphic 1.33x**
  - **SIRUI 35mm f/1.8 Anamorphic 1.33x**
  - **SIRUI 24mm f/2.8 Anamorphic 1.33x**
  - **Atlas Orion 40mm T2 2x Anamorphic**
  - **Atlas Orion 65mm T2 2x Anamorphic**

## Lens Accessories
- **Matte Box**
  - Tiltaing Mini Matte Box
- **Follow Focus**
  - Tilta Nucleus-Nano Wireless Follow Focus System
- **Lens Filters**
  - Tiffen Variable ND Filter
  - PolarPro QuartzLine ND Filters (ND8, ND16, ND32)
  - Tiffen Black Pro-Mist Filter (1/4, 1/2)

## Camera Support
- **Tripod**
  - Manfrotto 504X Fluid Video Head with 645 Fast Twin Tripod
- **Gimbal**
  - DJI Ronin-S
- **Shoulder Rig**
  - Tilta Shoulder Rig Kit for BMPCC4K

## Lighting
- **Key Lights**
  - Aputure 120d II with Light Dome II
  - Godox SL60W
- **Fill Lights**
  - Aputure Amaran AL-F7
  - Neewer 660 LED Video Light
- **Practical Lights**
  - Various LED bulbs and practical lamps for interior scenes
- **Modifiers**
  - Softboxes, diffusers, and reflectors

## Audio
- **Microphones**
  - Rode NTG4+ Shotgun Microphone
  - Sennheiser MKE 600 Shotgun Microphone
  - Zoom H5 Handy Recorder
- **Lavalier Mics**
  - Rode Wireless GO II
- **Boom Pole**
  - K-Tek KE-89CC Avalon Series Aluminum Boom Pole

## Monitoring
- **External Monitor**
  - Atomos Ninja V 5" 4K HDMI Recording Monitor
- **Wireless Video Transmission**
  - Hollyland Mars 400S Pro

## Power
- **Batteries**
  - Multiple LP-E6 Batteries for BMPCC4K
  - V-Mount Batteries (e.g., Anton Bauer Titon 90)
- **Battery Chargers**
  - Dual LP-E6 Battery Charger
  - V-Mount Battery Charger

## Storage
- **Memory Cards**
  - SanDisk Extreme Pro 128GB CFast 2.0 Cards
  - Samsung T5 1TB SSD (for external recording)

  - Lexar Professional USB 3.0 Dual-Slot Reader

## Miscellaneous
- **Clapperboard**
  - Traditional Clapperboard
- **Slate**
  - Digital Slate App (e.g., MovieSlate 8)
- **Gaffer Tape**
  - Black and white gaffer tape
- **Sandbags**
  - Multiple sandbags for stabilizing equipment
- **Lens Cleaning Kit**
  - Microfiber cloths, lens cleaning solution, air blower

## Post-Production
- **Editing Software**
  - DaVinci Resolve Studio
- **Color Grading**
  - DaVinci Resolve Studio
- **Sound Design**
  - Adobe Audition or Pro Tools

This list should cover the essential equipment needed to shoot "Sea Monster" with a BMPCC4K and anamorphic lenses. Adjustments can be made based on specific scene requirements and budget constraints.
