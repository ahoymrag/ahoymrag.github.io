    • Budget Overview ($7,500)

      To create a budget of $7,500, we'll need to prioritize essential elements and allocate funds accordingly. Here's a breakdown of the key areas we'll focus on:

      • Pre-Production: $2,500
      • Production: $3,000
      • Post-Production: $1,500
      • Marketing & Distribution: $500
    • Pre-Production Costs

      Pre-production costs include expenses related to script development, location scouting, casting, and scheduling. We'll allocate $2,500 for these activities.

    • Production Costs

      Production costs involve expenses related to filming, directing, sound recording, and lighting. We'll allocate $3,000 for these activities.

    • Post-Production Costs

      Post-production costs include expenses related to editing, sound editing, visual effects, color grading, and distribution. We'll allocate $1,500 for these activities.

    • Marketing & Distribution

      We'll allocate $500 for marketing and distribution efforts to ensure our film reaches a wider audience.

    • Cost-Saving Strategies

      To save costs, we'll consider the following strategies:

      • Use local crew and volunteers
      • Use free or low-cost locations
      • Use natural lighting
      • Use free or low-cost equipment
      • Use non-professional actors
    • Budget Planning

      We'll create a detailed budget plan that outlines all expenses and allocate funds accordingly.

    • Budget Approval

      We'll review and approve the budget plan with the production team and stakeholders.
