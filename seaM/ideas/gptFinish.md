
## THE STORM HITS DURING THE HARVEST FESTIVAL

EXT. TOWN SQUARE - HARVEST FESTIVAL - NIGHT

The HARVEST FESTIVAL is in full swing. Bright lights illuminate the booths and rides, but the atmosphere is tense. VENDORS and TOWNSPEOPLE move quickly, glancing up at the darkening sky. The storm is approaching--THUNDER RUMBLES in the distance, and the wind picks up speed.

Makayla, Mac, and Dallas are at the edge of the festival, eyes on the gathering clouds.

**MAKAYLA**
_(quietly to Mac)_
We have to go now. It's getting worse.

Mac nods, glancing at Dallas. There's an unspoken understanding between them.

**DALLAS**
_(to the kids)_
Go. I'll stay close by. If anything happens...

Makayla nods and grabs her BACKPACK, filled with the objects for the ritual. She and Mac slip away from the crowd, moving toward the tall grass, disappearing into the night. Dallas watches them go, his face tight with concern. The CARNIVAL MUSIC and SHOUTS from the festival fade into the background as he follows them at a distance.

## THE RITUAL BEGINS

EXT. TALL GRASS FIELD - NIGHT

The wind is howling now, the grass whipping violently in the storm. LIGHTNING FLASHES across the sky as Makayla and Mac reach the ritual site. The symbols they carved into the dirt earlier are still glowing faintly, flickering in and out as the storm intensifies.

**MAKAYLA**
_(urgently)_
We don't have much time.

They set down the STONES and OFFERINGS--each one tied to the town's past, items passed down through generations. Makayla places her GRANDFATHER'S COMPASS in the center of the circle, her hands trembling slightly.

**MAC**
_(nervously looking at the sky)_
What if this doesn't work?

Makayla glances at him, her resolve hardening.

**MAKAYLA**
It has to.

They begin the CHANT, their voices barely audible over the wind. The storm grows fiercer, RAIN starting to pour down in sheets.

## THE TOWN EVACUATES

EXT. TOWN SQUARE - CONTINUOUS

Back at the festival, the townspeople are starting to panic. TENTS are being ripped from their anchors, and the WIND sends debris flying. The WEATHER SERVICE van drives by, blaring evacuation orders.

**WEATHER SERVICE**
_(through loudspeakers)_
This is a mandatory evacuation! Leave the area immediately!

Dallas stands on the edge of the festival, his eyes fixed on the tall grass where the kids disappeared. He knows he should evacuate with the others, but something holds him back.

## THE CREATURES APPEAR

EXT. TALL GRASS FIELD - CONTINUOUS

As the kids continue the chant, the GLOWING EYES of the creatures begin to appear in the darkness once again. But this time, the creatures are larger, more defined, their forms towering over the field. Their MENACING FIGURES move through the storm, their presence amplified by the raging wind and lightning.

**MAKAYLA**
_(whispers, barely audible)_
They're here.

The creatures step closer, their EYES BURNING BRIGHTLY, and Makayla feels a surge of fear--but she doesn't stop chanting. The symbols in the dirt glow even brighter, and the wind seems to center around them.

## DALLAS'S VISION

EXT. TALL GRASS FIELD - CONTINUOUS

Dallas watches from a distance, the rain soaking him to the bone. His heart races as the creatures become visible, their forms barely human but unmistakably real. He's terrified for the kids but stays rooted to the spot, unable to look away.

As the ritual reaches its peak, the CREATURE LEADER steps forward, its eyes locking onto Dallas. Suddenly, Dallas feels a shift--a strange calm washes over him, and he's transported into a VISION.

## VISION:

EXT. PARK - DAY (DALLAS'S MEMORY)

Dallas stands in a familiar park. It's sunny and warm, the wind gentle. MAC'S MOTHER, Dallas's wife, stands in front of him, smiling--just as she did the last time they were here. Her laughter echoes in the air, and for a moment, everything feels peaceful again.

**DALLAS**
_(whispers, tears welling up)_
I miss you.

She steps forward, placing her hand on his cheek. Her touch feels real, warm.

**DALLAS'S WIFE**
_(softly)_
Take care of him, Dallas. You'll be okay.

Before he can respond, the vision starts to fade. The storm's sound begins to seep back in, and his wife's figure starts to dissolve into the wind.

## BACK TO REALITY

EXT. TALL GRASS FIELD - BACK TO PRESENT

Dallas is back in the field, but something has changed in him. His face is wet with rain--and with tears. The creatures are still watching, but their presence feels different now. Less threatening.

**DALLAS**
_(softly, to the creatures)_
I understand.

## MAKAYLA'S VISION

EXT. TALL GRASS FIELD - CONTINUOUS

Makayla's voice breaks slightly as she continues the chant. The creatures are closer now, almost surrounding them. Suddenly, she feels a strange pull in her chest, and everything around her STOPS. The wind, the rain--everything goes still.

**GRANDFATHER (V.O.)**
_(softly)_
Makayla.

She gasps, recognizing the voice immediately. She looks around, and there, standing at the edge of the circle, is her GRANDFATHER, his form faint but unmistakable. Tears fill her eyes as she stares at him.

**MAKAYLA**
_(voice trembling)_
Grandpa...?

He steps forward, his eyes gentle but filled with urgency.

**GRANDFATHER**
The balance, Makayla. You have to finish it.

Makayla swallows hard, nodding. She feels the weight of her responsibility, but also the love and support of her grandfather, as if he's guiding her through the ritual.

## THE RITUAL CONCLUDES

EXT. TALL GRASS FIELD - CONTINUOUS

The storm resumes in full force, the creatures' glowing eyes watching as Makayla takes a deep breath and continues the chant, her voice stronger now.

**MAKAYLA**
_(chanting louder)_
We restore the balance. We protect the land.

The CREATURE LEADER steps forward once more, raising its arm toward the sky. The storm around them seems to respond--the wind shifts, and the rain lessens.

Dallas, still watching, feels a sense of calm wash over him again. The visions, the creatures, the storm--it's all connected, tied to the town and its history.

## THE STORM SUBSIDES

EXT. TALL GRASS FIELD - CONTINUOUS

As Makayla finishes the final words of the chant, the creatures seem to glow brighter, their forms shimmering in the stormy night. Then, one by one, they start to DISSIPATE, fading into the wind, leaving behind only the faintest trace of their presence.

The storm begins to SUBSIDE, the wind slowing to a gentle breeze. The rain lightens, and the sky, once filled with dark clouds, starts to CLEAR.

Makayla, Mac, and Dallas stand in the TALL GRASS, breathing heavily, their faces a mix of relief and awe. They did it. The ritual worked.

## AFTERMATH

EXT. TOWN SQUARE - LATER

The HARVEST FESTIVAL has been abandoned, the rides and booths standing eerily still in the aftermath of the storm. But the town is safe. The hurricane has passed.

Dallas and the kids walk back into the town, drenched and exhausted, but there's a sense of peace in the air. The storm may be over, but they all know that the creatures will always be watching, maintaining the balance.

**MAKAYLA**
_(softly, to herself)_
We did it, Grandpa.

She glances up at the sky, and for just a moment, she swears she sees her grandfather's face in the clouds, smiling down at her.

## THE CREATURE SAVES THE TOWN

EXT. TALL GRASS FIELD - NIGHT

The WIND is still swirling around the kids and DALLAS, but it's starting to shift, slowing down as the ritual nears completion. MAKAYLA stands at the center of the circle, the SYMBOLS glowing brightly beneath her feet. MAC and DALLAS look on, both exhausted and amazed.

The CREATURES that surrounded them are beginning to react. Their GLOWING EYES intensify as they step forward, their forms towering above the field. But now, there's no menace in their presence--only a quiet power.

**MAKAYLA**
_(chanting, voice rising)_
Restore the balance. Protect the land.

**CREATURE LEADER**
_(without words, through presence)_
We hear you.

Suddenly, the CREATURES lift their arms as one, and a surge of energy bursts forth from them. The air around them SHIMMERS, and the ground beneath them vibrates. LIGHTNING crackles across the sky, but instead of destruction, it feels like the energy is healing the land.

## THE STORM CALMS

EXT. TALL GRASS FIELD - CONTINUOUS

As the creatures release the energy, the storm begins to CALM. The WIND dies down, the RAIN becomes a gentle drizzle, and the THUNDER quiets in the distance. The SKY, once dark and menacing, begins to CLEAR. Faint stars peek through the clouds.

**MAC**
_(whispering, in awe)_
It's working.

Makayla lowers her arms, her face lit by the faint glow of the symbols. The CREATURES slowly begin to FADE, their forms dissolving into the mist. One by one, they DISAPPEAR into the tall grass, leaving behind only their GLOWING EYES, watching over the field.

## AFTERMATH

EXT. TALL GRASS FIELD - LATER

The storm has passed. MAKAYLA, MAC, and DALLAS stand together, drenched and exhausted, but there's a sense of peace in the air. The town has been saved, and they've restored the balance.

The GLOWING EYES of the creatures linger in the distance for a moment longer, a quiet acknowledgment of their work. Then, finally, they too disappear, leaving the field quiet and still.

Makayla wipes rain from her face, her eyes distant as she looks out at the empty field. Dallas steps up beside her, placing a hand on her shoulder. There's a heaviness in his eyes, but also relief.

**DALLAS**
_(softly)_
You did it, Makayla.

Makayla turns to look at him, her face filled with quiet pride and emotion.

**MAKAYLA**
_(quietly)_
We all did.

She glances up at the sky, where the last remnants of the storm clouds are clearing, revealing the stars. For a brief moment, she swears she sees her GRANDFATHER'S FACE, smiling down at her, but it quickly fades into the night.

## RETURNING TO THE FESTIVAL

EXT. HARVEST FESTIVAL - NIGHT

Back in the TOWN SQUARE, the HARVEST FESTIVAL continues in the aftermath of the storm. People are slowly returning to the festival grounds, helping to pick up debris and reset the booths. There's a sense of quiet celebration now--the town is safe, and life goes on.

MAKAYLA, MAC, and DALLAS walk back into the square, their clothes still soaked but their faces filled with relief. They're greeted with curious glances from the townspeople, who have no idea what just happened, but for the kids, everything feels different.

**MAKAYLA**
_(looking around, softly)_
It feels like... something's changed.

Mac nods, looking at the familiar sights of the festival--the booths, the games, the families laughing in the distance.

**MAC**
_(quietly)_
We know something they don't.

Dallas stands beside them, his gaze distant as he watches the people celebrate. His mind drifts back to the VISION OF HIS WIFE from the ritual. The emotion of the moment washes over him, and he blinks back tears.

**DALLAS**
_(softly, almost to himself)_
I think they're watching over us now.

Makayla looks up at him, sensing the deeper meaning behind his words. She doesn't say anything, but she gives him a small, understanding nod.

## CELEBRATION RESUMES

EXT. TOWN SQUARE - LATER

The town has resumed its FESTIVAL SPIRIT. The lights are back on, the music is playing again, and the people are laughing and celebrating like nothing ever happened. But for Makayla, Mac, and Dallas, the night holds a deeper significance.

The kids stand on the edge of the square, watching the festivities with quiet smiles.

**MAKAYLA**
_(to Mac, softly)_
We did something important tonight.

Mac nods, glancing at her with a newfound respect.

**MAC**
_(quietly)_
We saved the town. Just like your grandpa would've done.

Makayla's face softens at the mention of her grandfather. She reaches into her pocket and pulls out his COMPASS, running her fingers over the familiar object.

**MAKAYLA**
_(whispers, smiling)_
Yeah. Just like he would've done.

She looks up at the sky one last time, her heart filled with peace. The stars twinkle above, and the storm is nothing but a memory now.
