# Dallas Likes


## General Information
- **Name:** Dallas
- **Age:** 40
- **Family:** Son of Henry and Georgetta
- **Location:** Recently moved to the Southern Connecticut Coast from Idaho

## Personality Traits
- **Curious:** Mac is often seen observing his surroundings, whether it's the new house, the tall grass, or the mysterious events happening around him.
- **Nervous/Anxious:** He shows signs of nervousness and uncertainty, especially in new situations like starting at a new school or encountering strange phenomena.
- **Brave:** Despite his fears, Mac is willing to explore and face the unknown, especially when it comes to the mysterious creatures.
- **Observant:** Mac notices small details, such as the burn marks near the mailbox and the eyes in the tall grass.

## Relationships
- **Dominic (Neighbor):** Dominic is Dallas's Culd-a-sac Neighbor - - Mac has a close relationship with his father. They share moments of concern and reassurance, especially regarding the strange events happening around them.
- **Makayla:** Mac is intrigued by Makayla and her stories about the creatures. He trusts her and is willing to join her in her investigations.
- **Asher:** Mac and Asher share a bond through their mutual curiosity and involvement in the search for the creatures.
