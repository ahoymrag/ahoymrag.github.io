The brothers agreed to help clean up the boat - glue it where it needed to be glued - and doze it with a fresh coat of extra paint. 

I watched the brothers as they danced about in harmony - I stepped back and entered the back of the mind and minded all of the other moving pieces in play. I checked each individual status of 10 second-hand metal detectors and I wondered if I calculated the fact that some may need new batteries. How long are we scanning the sand?. This two day rental of this airbnb we found at the Thimble - with the condition written in the guest request section on the website - that if we happened to find gold coins outside in the sand - it would be ours to keep.
