Executive Summary

This business plan outlines the strategy to launch a vending machine operation in New Haven, CT, with a starting budget of $10,000. The objective is to achieve a monthly revenue of $5,000 within three months of operation. The business will initially focus on strategically placing five vending machines in high-traffic areas, offering a mix of snacks and beverages.
