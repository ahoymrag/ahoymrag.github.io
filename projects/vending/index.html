Executive Summary

This business plan outlines the strategy to launch a vending machine operation in New Haven, CT, with a starting budget of $10,000. The objective is to achieve a monthly revenue of $5,000 within three months of operation. The business will initially focus on strategically placing five vending machines in high-traffic areas, offering a mix of snacks and beverages.

Business Overview

  • Name: New Haven Vending Solutions
  • Services: Providing snack and beverage vending machines.
  • Target Market: High foot traffic areas such as office buildings, educational institutions, hospitals, and transportation hubs.
  • Goals: Achieve a monthly revenue of $5,000 within the first three months.

Conclusion

New Haven Vending Solutions aims to establish a profitable vending machine business in New Haven, CT, starting with a strategic approach to location, inventory, and customer satisfaction. The goal is to create a sustainable business model that can expand with reinvestment of profits and potentially diversify into new vending machine technologies or markets.
