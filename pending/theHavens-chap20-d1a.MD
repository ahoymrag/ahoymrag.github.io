## Chapter 20: The Start of the Treasure Hunt

So, by and by, as the days grew - so did Kenny’s Lewis thoughts. 
Kenny Lewis didn’t care about the wealth or the acclaim no - nor was he pressed by grandeur or self acclaim - lest at first - no - what Kenny Lewis really wanted. More than any Gold - was the adventure - the chance to use his gadgets among his friends. 

And I really wanted that too - I wanted something to do - something to call my own. Life as an Elderman was sparsely demanding - and I needed something to get me out of this slunk. 

Knowing that Kenny Lewis would never assemble such a crew  - nor that he would admit that he needed help at all - as - so I made short work of it. 
We needed 
Someone with a boat
Someone who knew how to use the boat
Someone who knew where to look - based on maps and such 
Someone to help us take the raw materials off the island - into the boat - transport it from Branford to Beathany and make some route along 95. 
Someone to help with security - 

I pinned up the first two items as things to worry about later. What I was most interested in was someone who knew how to read maps. Unfortunately, the only person I associated with maps - was an elderly Librarian woman I befriended while working out of the library - Deb Miller - Deb once showed me a collection of New Haven maps. Perhaps she had one of the thimble island. 

The other problem we faced early on - was detection. I didn’t know the legalities exactly - so I consulted my friend Brenn, who was some hot shot law student out of quinipaic who had a chip on his shoulder as he wasn’t going to Yale but still lived in East Rock. 
 
The more people I added - the more possibilities of why would anyone help us - I wasn’t planning on sharing any of the loot - nor wanted to make any promises. If it were up to me - the only ones who would have the loot - were the looters who formulated the plan for the loot in the first place - mainly Kenny Lewis and myself. Kenny would take 70 percent - I would take 30 - I figured that was a fair, humble number. 

The first order of business was to assemble a time and place to meet. Least to msyelf  - I needed to have something preparred before hand - as one simply cannot invite people without having a place for them to get excited about going. 

I figured the best place to start would be somewhere everyone knew. I decided that the rooftop of Elm City Social - could be an option - but when I called to ask they said that the rooftop only opens after labor day - so the search continued.
