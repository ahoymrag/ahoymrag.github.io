Forest Frenzy: A Parkour Racing Adventure

Game Overview

Forest Frenzy combines the high-speed racing excitement of Mario Kart, the fluid parkour mechanics of Mirror's Edge, and the creative freedom of Skate into a unique multiplayer experience. Set in a vibrant, interactive forest world, players choose between agile squirrels and bouncy bunnies, engaging in thrilling races through dynamic, obstacle-laden courses. With an emphasis on skillful navigation and strategic power-ups, Forest Frenzy promises heart-pounding action and endless fun.

Gameplay Mechanics Overview

Forest Frenzy's gameplay is centered around parkour-inspired movement, allowing players to run, jump, climb, and use the environment to gain speed and outmaneuver opponents. Each race is filled with shortcuts, hazards, and interactive elements that can either provide an advantage or pose a challenge.

  • Characters: Choose from a variety of squirrel and bunny racers, each with unique stats and abilities.
  • Lore: Each critter in the game has its own unique backstory and place in the forest's ecosystem.
  • Dynamic Courses: Race through ever-changing courses that adapt to player actions, ensuring no two races are the same.
  • Multiplayer: Compete in online multiplayer races or team up with friends to tackle specially designed co-op challenges.

Technical Details

Utilizing a low-poly model suitable for various genres, Forest Frenzy is designed to deliver a visually stunning yet performance-friendly experience. The character model includes 13 animations (walk, run, idle, jump, sitdown, sitting, standup, look, climb, sleep) to support a wide range of movement and interaction within the game world.

  • Vertex Count: 5321
  • Texture Resolution: 4096x4096
  • Animations: 13
  • Supported Platforms: Windows, Mac
