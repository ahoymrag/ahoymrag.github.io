# Action Plan

## Overview of Tasks and Timeline

### Goal: Acquire Land and Build a Basic Campsite by Thanksgiving 2024

#### Tasks:
1. **Research and Identify Potential Land Locations**
   - Use websites like Zillow, Realtor.com, and local real estate listings.
   - Contact real estate agents for site visits and additional information.

2. **Conduct Site Surveys and Finalize Land Acquisition Details**
   - Visit potential land sites with real estate agents.
   - Review and compare site options.

3. **Negotiate and Finalize Land Purchase**
   - Begin negotiations and legal documentation for land purchase.
   - Account for closing costs and time.
   - Ensure all legal documentation is in order.

4. **Begin Permit Application Process**
   - Start early to avoid delays.
   - Follow up on permit applications and approvals.
   - Account for the time required for permit approval in the Berkshires, MA.

5. **Prepare the Site for a Basic Campsite**
   - Initial site preparation for a basic campsite.
   - Set up a tiny home or temporary shelter for winter use.

6. **Build a Basic Campsite**
   - Set up essential utilities (if possible).
   - Ensure the campsite is ready for winter use.

7. **Plan for Early Spring Construction**
   - Develop detailed construction plans for early spring.
   - Coordinate with contractors and suppliers for a smooth start.

#### Timeline:
- **Start Date**: September 30, 2024
- **End Date**: November 28, 2024 (Thanksgiving)
- **Total Days Available**: 60 days (excluding the holiday break from October 4-8)
