# Budget and Overview for Land Acquisition and Wigwam Yurt Style Editing Suites

**Total Budget**: $30,000

## 1. Land Acquisition
- **Land Purchase**: $10,000
  - 1 acre of rural land suitable for building and filming.

## 2. Permits and Legal Fees
- **Permits**: $1,000
  - Building permits, zoning permits, and other necessary legal documentation.
- **Legal Fees**: $500
  - Lawyer fees for land purchase and permit applications.

## 3. Construction of Wigwam Yurt Style Editing Suites
- **Materials**: $8,000
  - Wood, canvas, insulation, and other building materials.
- **Labor**: $4,000
  - Skilled labor for construction, including carpenters and electricians.
- **Utilities Setup**: $2,000
  - Installation of basic utilities such as electricity, water, and internet.

## 4. Interior Setup
- **Editing Equipment**: $3,000
  - Computers, monitors, editing software (e.g., DaVinci Resolve, Adobe Premiere), and peripherals.
- **Furniture**: $1,000
  - Desks, chairs, and storage units.
- **Climate Control**: $500
  - Basic heating and cooling solutions.

## 5. Miscellaneous
- **Contingency Fund**: $1,000
  - For unexpected expenses and overruns.

---

**Total**: $30,000

---

## Overview

### Land Acquisition
The first step is to acquire 1 acre of rural land that is suitable for building and filming. This land will serve as the foundation for our editing suites and provide a serene environment conducive to creative work.

### Permits and Legal Fees
Securing the necessary permits and handling legal documentation is crucial to ensure that the construction and operation of the editing suites comply with local regulations.

### Construction of Wigwam Yurt Style Editing Suites
The editing suites will be constructed in a unique wigwam yurt style, providing a rustic yet functional workspace. The construction will involve sourcing quality materials and hiring skilled labor to build and set up the structures.

### Interior Setup
The interior of the editing suites will be equipped with state-of-the-art editing equipment and comfortable furniture to create an efficient and pleasant working environment. Basic climate control will be installed to ensure comfort throughout the year.

### Miscellaneous
A contingency fund is allocated to cover any unexpected expenses that may arise during the project.

---

**Note**: This budget is a high-level estimate and may require adjustments based on actual costs and availability of resources. The project relies on careful planning and efficient use of funds to stay within the $30,000 budget.
