Set in the Caribbean, "Maybe Love" is part of a 40,000 word Women's Fiction collection called Maybe Baby that reinforces its protagonists' middle school, high school, and post-graduate coming of age stories. In "Maybe Love," 14 year old Annika and her family live on board a catamaran bound for St. Kitts during an extended gap year. When it's finally time to return home, Annika is determined to keep her new found life no matter what her parents say, even if it means losing herself along the way. Each of the three Maybe stories wrestle with the uncertainty of transformation and the bittersweet realization that what it means to be alive isn't always what it seems.
